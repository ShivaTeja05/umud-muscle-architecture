Calibration first

Order of work

  1. Calibration (12 images, ids CAL_01–CAL_12, violet in the grid). These are public benchmark images with known expert answers, which stay hidden. Measure them exactly like test images. They tell us your personal error and bias per parameter. Four of them are frames of a calf-raise video: there only FL and PA are needed, MT is optional.
  2. Telemed clips (28 tasks, IMG_00056–00195, first frame of each clip).
  3. The other test images, only if there is time.

Where to click (schematic)

  • MT: lines between the inner edges (the dark muscle side) of the two aponeuroses, never through the bright aponeurosis itself.
  • FL: prefer fascicles in the middle of the image that need the least extrapolation; extend the line straight past the image edge when needed.
  • PA: two clicks on the fascicle close to where it meets the deep aponeurosis, then two clicks along the deep aponeurosis right there.

Protocol (organiser, UMUD test labels)

  • Superficial muscle only: the top (superficial) aponeurosis and the first deep aponeurosis below it. Ignore deeper muscles.
  • MT ×3: vertical lines (global image vertical) at the left, middle and right of the image. Click the inner edge of the superficial aponeurosis, then the inner edge of the deep aponeurosis. x is locked to the first click.
  • FL ×3: trace each fascicle as a segmented line from the deep to the superficial aponeurosis, following its curvature. Extend by hand beyond the image if needed (click outside the image). Choose fascicles that need the least extrapolation (near the image centre). Double-click or Enter ends a fascicle.
  • PA ×3: angle at the insertion on the deep aponeurosis. Click 2 points along a fascicle close to its insertion, then 2 points along the deep aponeurosis at that insertion. Use different fascicles from the FL ones.
  • Each parameter is the mean of its 3 values. Scale: the vertical scale bar, applied to both axes (square pixels, even on stretched 1200×800 images). The tool does this for you.
  • Clips IMG_00056–00195: only the first frame of each 5-frame clip is a task; its values are copied to all 5 frames on export. Use , . to flick through the frames (motion helps to see fascicles).

Mouse

  • Click: place a point. Drag (any button) or Space+drag: pan. Wheel / pinch: zoom at cursor.
  • Drag an existing point of this image to adjust it (hover turns it white).
