# Review and plan toward 0.20 — 22 October 2026

The verified baseline is **C18, public score 0.33526**. Kaggle account submission
history was checked during this review; no newer candidate has a score. Reaching
0.20 requires a reduction of **0.13526, or 40.3% of the current score**. This is an
experimental target, not a result we can promise from the available evidence.

The subsequent paired fold-0 GPU pilot has now completed. See
[measured results and next work](curve_pilot_results_2026-10-22.md): track and
deep-strip errors improved, while the PA proxy did not. No new Kaggle score was
obtained. The review below records the rationale behind that experiment.

## What the previous work established

Reviewed the source, training kernels, saved experiment tables, candidates v1–v8,
Runpod results, and the preserved Claude reports. The progression is:

| Experiment | Public score | What it tells us |
|---|---:|---|
| Old calibrated U-Net++ | 0.92533 | Small-benchmark calibration did not transfer. |
| Corrected geometry v1 | 0.46718 | Scale, anatomy and measurement definitions mattered substantially. |
| C1 geometry v2 | 0.45436 | Further geometry cleanup helped. |
| C2, shrinkage on older maps | 0.38974 | Reducing noisy predictions helped that pipeline. |
| C12, orientation field and later assembly | 0.37670 | A further gain, smaller than local proxy scores suggested. |
| C11, group-constant PA/FL | 0.51239 | Replacing individual measurements with group constants was harmful. |
| C13, shorter Telemed FL | 0.41184 | This benchmark-favoured length estimator did not transfer. |
| C15, same estimator with improved apo maps | 0.41182 | The new maps barely changed this public result. This comparison does not isolate every possible use of those maps. |
| C17, rater offsets removed | 0.38091 | Removing the existing offsets hurt this public result. |
| **C18, raw Telemed clip FL** | **0.33526** | Trusting these individual length estimates was a large improvement. |
| C19, Telemed triangle coupling | 0.41013 | Enforcing a population PA/FL/MT relationship was harmful. |

These are observed file-level results. Prior reports' scanner/component error
decompositions were fitted to leaderboard totals and rely on assumptions; they
are **not measured hidden-label errors**. Likewise, the inter-rater benchmark
does not establish a hard noise floor for this competition.

The unscored v7/v8 files remain hypotheses. In particular, the physical-aspect
probes contradict the image-space interpretation documented in the organizer
discussion notes. I would not treat their hypothetical gains as a route already
established to 0.20. The small shrinkage probes also cannot reasonably account
for the entire gap.

## Next experiment: curved-track supervision

The previous v2 round mainly reused the model's own predictions. The next
intervention supplies genuinely different supervision: the public expert-drawn
VL fascicle tracks already embedded in the training code. The earlier optional
VL-supervised variant was planned but has not been evaluated in the saved runs.

The paired pilots are generated under
`artifacts/gpu_experiments/curve_v3_pilot`:

- **control:** v1 fold-0 initialization, existing v2 augmentation, GT fragments.
- **curves:** identical initialization and augmentation, plus curved-track
  orientation supervision from VL subjects outside fold 0.

Both disable ensemble pseudo-labels, use the same test-only style bank, the
same subject-held-out VL diagnostics, and four epochs. Extra supervised samples
give the curves arm more steps per epoch; this is an intervention in supervision,
not an exactly compute-matched architecture comparison. Both retain the existing
GT-fold checkpoint selection rule. If the selected model remains epoch 0, the
pilot reports that rather than claiming an improvement from an unselected epoch.

**Pilot gate:** verify CUDA training and checkpoint reload; compare control and
curves on the SAME held-out VL subjects and GT fold. Continue only if held-out
track and deep-strip angle errors improve without a material GT-fold regression.
A single fold is directional evidence; confirm on a second subject fold before
five-fold training. VL then becomes an in-domain, subject-held-out evaluation,
and must not be described as an unseen-scanner benchmark.

If that gate passes, test whether the learned curvature improves complete
fascicle paths and their endpoints. A lower angle loss alone is insufficient:
evaluate PA, FL and MT separately using the appropriate expert-label protocols.
Add an independent benchmark such as the previously scouted TimTrack data before
selecting among many new variants; verify its license and annotation conventions
at acquisition. Keep it out of training and model selection initially.

If curvature supervision does not transfer, stop that branch. The next branches
are image-supported aponeurosis boundary refinement and crop-aware endpoint
extrapolation. Avoid another broad self-training or backbone sweep until a
diagnostic identifies which measurement it is expected to improve.

## Compute and handoff

The first machine recommendation was **one A100 80 GB**, at least 64 GB system
RAM and about 100 GB disk. The supplied A100 has now completed both pilots:
approximately 7.3 minutes per arm and 6.1 GiB highest logged training allocation
at batch 8. Setup, smoke and follow-up diagnostics are additional; allocated
training memory is not total device memory.
An H100 is an alternative; the earlier project runs already exercised it.
Runpod's [pricing page](https://www.runpod.io/pricing), checked 22 October,
lists A100 80 GB at $1.79/hour and H100 PCIe at $2.89/hour; live stock, cloud tier
and storage can change the final cost. Allocate an initial **two GPU-hours** to
smoke tests and the paired pilot, then reassess from measured runtime and results.

Training time guards do not stop pod billing. Use an independent pod lifetime
limit, save checkpoints/output to persistent storage, retrieve the results, and
stop the machine afterward. The supplied pod was accessed with the explicitly
authorized `~/.ssh/runpod` key. Both arm outputs were retrieved and checksum
verified. See the results report for the final retrieval and pod status; finishing
the experiment does not itself stop Runpod billing.

The numerical target is substantial: for example, global MAEs of PA 1.2°, FL
2.4 mm and MT 0.6 mm give a main score of 0.20 under the 6°/12 mm/3 mm
tolerances. Those are an illustrative error budget, not measured current
component errors or a promised achievable combination.
